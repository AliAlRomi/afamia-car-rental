Clear rental information

Know the terms before you book.

Clear policies help avoid misunderstandings. Final rental conditions should always be confirmed in writing before the vehicle is collected.

Security Deposit

The deposit amount, refund method and expected processing period are confirmed before the rental begins.

Mileage Allowance

Included mileage and charges for additional kilometres are stated in the rental agreement.

Insurance and Damage

Insurance coverage, excess amounts and customer responsibilities are explained before confirmation.

Additional Charges

Delivery, fuel, fines, tolls, late return and other possible charges should be clearly listed.

Before you rent

Documents commonly required

  • Emirates ID or passport
  • Valid UAE or accepted international driving licence
  • Visa or entry documentation where applicable
  • Confirmed contact and payment details
